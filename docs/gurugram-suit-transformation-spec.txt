WILL & FEAR: Gurugram level, Lantern suit and transformation spec (from Hark)
Save this in ~/Projects/lantern/docs/ and build from it.

Next feature: Gurugram level, Lantern suit and transformation. Build on the current game. Rushville stays the default level, and ?level=gurugram loads the city. Player, pad, constructs, fear and sim/ are shared. Put the whole city under one root group so we can add a floating origin later.

A. Real map: tools/bake-osm.mjs
- Query the Overpass API (overpass-api.de with a kumi.systems fallback, and a proper User-Agent) for bbox 28.4865,77.0790,28.5035,77.0985. That covers DLF Cyber City, Cyber Hub and a stretch of NH-48. Fetch building, highway, railway (Rapid Metro), leisure/park, landuse, natural=water|wood and amenity points.
- Convert lat/lon to local metres around the origin 28.4950, 77.0890, and write compact public/osm/gurugram.json. Show "© OpenStreetMap contributors" in the HUD.

B. city/osmcity.js: make it feel real, not like blocks
- Building height: use height, then building:levels×3.3 m, then a fallback by type (office/commercial in Cyber City 45–110 m, residential 12–30 m, small shops 4–9 m). Add per-building variety in tone, setbacks and podiums.
- Materials:
  - Office towers: a glass curtain-wall shader (procedural mullion grid, env-map reflection and Fresnel, random lit windows at night).
  - Everything else: plaster or concrete with grime and AO.
  - Rooftops: Gurgaon clutter (black water tanks, AC units, dish antennas), all instanced.
- Streets:
  - Roads: ribbons with width by class, worn asphalt, lane paint, kerbs, footpaths and medians.
  - NH-48 and the Rapid Metro: on viaduct pillars where OSM says bridge or railway.
  - Street furniture: instanced street lights, trees (neem and peepal tones) along roads and in parks, parked cars, autos and barricades as dynamic Rapier props using sim/breakables.
- Atmosphere: NCR haze, with warm dusty fog, a softer sun and a hazy horizon. At night, sodium-orange streetlights, lit towers and the Cyber Hub glow.
- Physics: a static collider per building (a cuboid fitted to its footprint's oriented box, or convex if simple), plus road and terrain.
- Budget on Medium for an M5: at least 60 fps and under 300 draw calls. Merge geometry per material, instance everything repeated, and add LOD or culling for distant blocks.

C. suit/suit.js: the Lantern
- Body: a rigged humanoid (three.js examples/models/gltf/Xbot.glb, a Mixamo X Bot) with an original suit shader. A matte black base with layered dark-green panels, thin emissive green seams with a Fresnel rim, the lantern emblem as a chest decal from a canvas texture, and a domino mask with glowing eyes.
- First person: visible forearms and hands in the suit, and a glowing ring on the right middle finger. ringTip follows the ring bone.
- Third person: a toggle with V, plus automatically during the transformation.

D. The transformation (movie style)
- Trigger: speaking the oath at full charge, holding L1+R1 for 1 s, or the T key.
- 2.2 s sequence:
  1. The camera eases out to a third-person orbit.
  2. The ring flares, with a bloom spike, the lightbar ramping to green, the heartbeat haptic rising into a surge, and trigger tension building.
  3. The suit grows outward from the ring. A uFormRadius uniform expands from the ring's position, measured over the body. Its leading edge is a bright band of green energy lines with particles, and the civilian clothes dissolve behind it.
  4. The mask forms last.
  5. A shockwave pulse gives nearby props a radial impulse and flattens the grass.
  6. The camera returns to first person.
- Holding T plays the power-down in reverse. Allocate nothing per frame, and use a synthesised rising-whoosh audio.
